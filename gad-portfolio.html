About Me

Passionate builder,
curious learner

I'm Gad, a developer and IT student from Rwanda 🇷🇼. I fell in love with web development because it sits at the intersection of logic and creativity — you can build something from nothing and watch it come to life in a browser.

My foundation is in PHP, MySQL, and JavaScript — the full stack of building real, database-backed web applications. I'm drawn to how systems work under the hood: from relational database design to how browsers render a page.

I'm continuously learning and expanding into modern JavaScript, REST APIs, and software engineering principles. Every project is a chance to write better code than the one before.

Off the keyboard, I'm a central defender on the football pitch — reading the game, organizing the defense, staying composed under pressure. Turns out that mindset translates well to engineering too.
